The challenge

Food waste is an inventory problem hiding in plain sight.

Retail teams must balance freshness, availability, and margin while demand shifts faster than traditional replenishment processes can respond.

♻

Perishables expire unsold

Static ordering and delayed interventions can leave products at risk of spoilage before teams act.

▤

Demand is hard to anticipate

Promotions, local events, weather, seasonality, and price changes all affect near-term sales.

⇄

Decisions are fragmented

Markdowns, replenishment, transfers, and freshness checks often happen in separate workflows.

✦FRESHY connects the decisions: predict what will sell, understand what will expire, and recommend the best next action.
End-to-end workflow

From raw retail signals to supervised action.

One coordinated decision loop. Multiple specialist agents. A planner remains the final decision-maker before execution.

▤
01

Ingest retail data

Bring together historical sales, stock levels, deliveries, prices, promotions, shelf life, weather, local events, and recorded waste.

⌁
02

Forecast & assess freshness

Estimate short-term demand, remaining shelf life, and spoilage risk at SKU and store level.

✳
03

Specialist agents reason

Demand, freshness, markdown, replenishment, transfer, and corrective-action agents propose interventions.

⚙
04

Orchestrate one decision

Resolve conflicting recommendations against stock, expiry windows, business rules, and operational priorities.

♙
05

Human review

Planners inspect the evidence and can approve, modify, or reject each proposed action.

➤
06

Simulated execution

Approved actions flow into simulated store, point-of-sale, pricing, logistics, or replenishment interfaces.

↻
07

Track outcomes

Compare actual outcomes with forecasts and baseline inventory policies to evaluate performance.

⌁
08

Learn & improve

Feed results back into evaluation and tuning so recommendations can improve over time.

Multi-agent intelligence

Specialists work independently. The system decides together.

Each agent evaluates its own objective, then the orchestration layer reconciles the proposals into one actionable recommendation per affected SKU and store.

⌁Predict

Demand Forecasting Agent

Predicts near-term sales using historical patterns, trends, promotions, pricing, weather, and local context.

Output: Expected units sold · Forecast interval
◉Protect

Freshness Agent

Estimates remaining shelf life and ranks inventory by spoilage risk and urgency.

Output: Risk score · Expiry window
%Price

Dynamic Markdown Agent

Evaluates discount options to improve sell-through while considering margin and time to expiry.

Output: Markdown percentage · Start time
▣Balance

Replenishment Agent

Recommends order quantities that balance predicted demand, available inventory, lead time, and waste exposure.

Output: Order quantity · Delivery timing
⇄Redistribute

Inter-Store Transfer Agent

Finds potential donor and receiving stores to redirect excess stock toward stronger demand.

Output: Transfer quantity · Store pair
⚙Resolve

Corrective Actions Agent

Identifies operational interventions when inventory, demand, freshness, and service objectives conflict.

Output: Ranked action options · Rationale
⚙

Orchestration engine · the decision layer

Checks every proposal against real-world constraints and selects one evidence-backed action for each affected SKU and store.
